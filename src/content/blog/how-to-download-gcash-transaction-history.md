Your GCash transaction history is useful for budgeting, bookkeeping, proving income, or checking a
payment you don't recognise. This guide explains how to get it as a PDF and what to do with it next.

## Before you start

- Make sure the GCash app is updated to the latest version.
- GCash sends the statement **by email**, so use an email address you can open on your phone or
  computer.
- Decide which dates you need (for example, a month or a whole year).

## Step 1: Open your transactions

In the GCash app, go to **Transactions**, then tap **Request transaction history**.

## Step 2: Request the PDF for your date range

Check that the email address shown is correct (update it if it isn't), choose the dates you need,
and submit the request.

## Step 3: Find the PDF and its password

GCash emails the PDF to you, usually within about 5 minutes. Look for an email titled **"Your
GCash Transaction History"**.

The PDF is password-protected. Read [what the GCash statement PDF password is](/blog/gcash-statement-pdf-password)
before you try to open it.

## Step 4: Save the PDF somewhere you can find it

- On a phone, save it to your Files or Downloads folder.
- On a computer, save it to a folder you use for statements.
- Don't forward the PDF or its password to people you don't trust. It contains your full
  transaction details.

## Step 5: Turn it into a spreadsheet

Open the [GCash statement converter](/), choose the PDF, type its password, and download an Excel
or CSV file. Everything happens on your device; the file is never uploaded. For a full walkthrough
see [how to convert a GCash statement to Excel](/blog/gcash-statement-to-excel-guide).

## Common problems

- **I didn't receive the statement.** Check your Spam or Junk folder. If it still isn't there,
  make sure your email address is correct and request it again.
- **The PDF won't open.** It needs its password. See the
  [GCash statement password guide](/blog/gcash-statement-pdf-password).
- **Something else isn't working.** Visit the GCash Help Center at
  [help.gcash.com](https://help.gcash.com).
